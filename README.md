# STUDYMART - WEBSITE BAN DO DUNG HOC TAP

Du an xay dung website thuong mai dien tu chuyen cung cap cac loai do dung hoc tap, ho tro khach hang mua sam truc tuyen va cung cap trang quan tri (Admin) de quan ly san pham, danh muc va don hang.

---

## 1. Thanh vien nhom thuc hien
* Du an thuc hien trong khuon khổ 04 tuan lam việc theo phuong phap Agile Scrum.

---

## 2. Cong nghe su dung
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, Mongoose
* **Database:** MongoDB local, MongoDB Compass
* **Ngon ngu:** JavaScript

---

## 3. Cau truc co so du lieu (05 Collections)
1. **users:** Quan ly thong tin tai khoan khach hang va phan quyen Admin.
2. **categories:** Quan ly danh muc cac mat hang do dung hoc tap.
3. **products:** Quan ly thong tin chi tiet san pham, gia ban va so luong ton kho.
4. **orders:** Quan ly thong tin don hang va trang thai thanh toan/giao hang.
5. **order_details:** Luu tru chi tiet san pham thuoc tung don hang.

---

## 4. Ke hoach trien khai (04 Tuan)
* **Sprint 1 (Tuan 1 & 2):** Phan tich he thong, thiet ke CSDL, ket noi MongoDB, xay dung Backend co ban, chuc nang tai khoan (Dang ky/Dang nhap), trang chu va quan ly san pham.
* **Sprint 2 (Tuan 3 & 4):** Xay dung chuc nang tim kiem, loc san pham, chi tiet san pham, gio hang, thanh toan, quan ly don hang cho Admin va Dashboard thong ke.S
